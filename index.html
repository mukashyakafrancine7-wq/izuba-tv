Gahunda z'Ibiganiro (TV & Radio)

Izuba TV na Radio bikora amasaha 24/7 bikagera ku baturage bo mu Karere ka Nyagatare, Gatsibo, Kayonza, Rwamagana, Ngoma, Kirehe na Bugesera. Ikerekezo cyacu ni gukorera abaturage amakuru y'ukuri, y'ingirakamaro no kubagenera imyidagaduro myiza.
